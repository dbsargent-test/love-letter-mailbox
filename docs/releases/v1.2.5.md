# Firmware v1.2.5

Build date: 2026-09-17

## Highlights

- Landscape 320x240 display layout.
- Floating-hearts screensaver after two minutes of inactivity.
- Photo prefetch before notification and first reveal.
- Two-slot LRU photo cache with a 512 KB per-image limit.
- Single-press next, double-press back, and one-second hold to mark read.
- Authenticated OTA polling every 15 minutes.
